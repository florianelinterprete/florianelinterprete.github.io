### How does a logistic company use reinforement learning to optimize delivery routes using Azure?
{: .faq-question}

A logistics company uses Reinforcement Learning in Azure Machine Learning to optimize delivery routes.
1. Delivery speed: The AI receives positive rewards for faster deliveries and negative rewards for delays.
2. Route selection: The AI tests different routes and learns which ones result in the fastest deliveries.
3. Delivery sequence: The AI experiments with different orders of visiting customers to minimize total delivery time.
4. Departure time: The AI learns which departure times result in the fastest journeys.
Why can AI outperform a human? It can run thousands of simulations, test far more route combinations, and learn from experience without getting tired or relying on intuition.
